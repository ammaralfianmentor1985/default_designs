# Keyword Map — Annie's Villa & Laviana Bungalow (Lovina)

Starter research by the SEO Manager (verified against live Google results, Oct 2025). Volumes in Lovina's niche are small but high-intent — a booking is worth ~US$30–60+/night, so even 10 visits/day of the right traffic matters. Once Search Console has data (Phase 5), this map gets re-ranked by real queries.

**Language:** English first (international guests; Lovina draws many German, Dutch and French travelers — their trip research is usually in English). Indonesian page set is a later phase.

## Tier 1 — Branded (win first; weeks)

| Keyword | Intent | Lands on |
|---|---|---|
| annie's villa lovina | Direct booking/check | Home |
| annies villa bali / annie villa lovina | Spelling variants | Home |
| laviana bungalow | Direct booking/check | `/laviana-bungalow/` |
| laviana bungalow lovina / airbnb | Variant | `/laviana-bungalow/` |
| annie's villa lovina reviews | Trust check | Home (reviews section) |

Notes: "laviana" alone is contested by **The Laviana Hotel** (different business, same street) — always write the full names "Annie's Villa Lovina" and "Laviana Bungalow at Annie's Villa" in titles, GBP, and directories.

## Tier 4 — Question keywords (FAQ page, `FAQPage` schema)

Already half-written in the design's FAQ section:
- Is the pool private to the guests?
- Can we rent the whole property? / group stays
- Are tours included in the price?
- How far is the market? / the beach / the hot springs
- Add: Do you offer airport pickup? · Is breakfast included? · Is Annie's Villa family-friendly? · Can you help with visa extension? (the Airbnb listing already advertises this — unusual and searchable)

## Rules for all pages

1. One primary keyword per page; put it in `<title>`, meta description, URL, and naturally once in body text.
2. Never stuff headings — the brand voice stays ("A quiet green villa").
3. Every guide links to Home + one unit page; Home links to every guide (see `site-structure.md`).
4. Re-rank this map monthly from Search Console query data.
