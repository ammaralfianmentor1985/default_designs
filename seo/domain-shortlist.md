# Domain Shortlist & Brand Naming

## Brand recommendation first

**Primary brand: "Annie's Villa Lovina".** Laviana Bungalow stays as a *unit* sub-brand ("Laviana Bungalow at Annie's Villa"). Reasons:
1. "Annie's Villa" is warmer, more memorable, and matches the designed landing page.
2. "Laviana" is contested — **The Laviana Hotel** is a different business on the same street; building the main brand on "Laviana" would feed their search results (and theirs would feed confusion into ours).
3. One domain + one Google Business Profile for the property; the bungalow gets its own page, not its own site.

## Domain candidates (in order of preference)

| Domain | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **anniesvillalovina.com** ⭐ | Brand + location keyword; unambiguous; matches GBP name | 18 chars, but reads cleanly |
| anniesvilla.com | Shortest | Likely taken/premium; no location |
| anniesvillabali.com | Brand + island keyword | "Bali" weaker than "Lovina" for local pack |
| villaannielovina.com | Keyword-first | Reads less naturally |
| lavianabungalow.com | Exact-match for the second brand | Collides with The Laviana Hotel; wrong primary brand |
| anniesvilla.id / .co.id | Indonesian identity | Intl guests trust .com more; .id costs more |

**Buy:** `anniesvillalovina.com`. Optionally also `lavianabungalow.com` (~$12/yr) purely as a redirect to `/laviana-bungalow/` — cheap brand protection, not required.

## How to buy (10 minutes, ~US$10–13/year)

1. Go to a registrar — **Cloudflare Registrar** (at-cost pricing) or **Namecheap** (easiest).
2. Search `anniesvillalovina.com` → if taken, go down the table; tell the SEO Manager chat what you bought.
3. Buy 1 year with auto-renew ON (an expired domain = the website and rankings vanish).
4. Turn ON free WHOIS privacy (default at both registrars).
5. Don't buy add-ons (email hosting, "SEO services", SSL — GitHub Pages gives SSL free).
6. Afterwards the Web Engineer chat gives you the exact DNS records (4 × A records + CNAME) to paste in — a 5-minute step with screenshots.

## Rules once chosen

- The domain goes in: GBP website field, Airbnb listing, WhatsApp Business profile, Instagram/Facebook bio, email signature.
- Never change the domain later without redirects — rankings reset.
